    Features

    80Gbps Ultra-Fast USB4 Connection

    Delivers up to 80Gbps bandwidth* with USB4 v2 (Thunderbolt 5 compatible) for true PCIe Gen 4 x4 performance. Ideal for AI and machine learning accelerators and ultra-fast NVMe storage. Consistent speed with no throttling or drop-offs.

    *PCIe data transfer speed is up to 64Gbps

    Supports PCIe Gen4/3 Devices, up to 64Gbps (Gen4 x4)

    Delivers full-speed PCIe Gen 4 and Gen 3 NVMe performance over USB4, giving you the fastest possible transfer rates for professional workflows and large file handling.

    Broad M.2 Size Compatibility

    Supports multiple M.2 seizes, including, 2230, 2242, 2260, and 2280, so you can choose the right drive your system - from compact SSDs to high-capacity options.

    Built for AI and PCIe Expansion

    More than storage, this enclosure supports high-performance PCIe modules such as AI accelerators and networking cards to add computing power or specialized capabilities to your system. Designed for engineers, developers, and creators who need extra performance without replacing their hardware.

    External Power Support

    Provides up to 18W of optional external USB-C power-in to support high-performance NVMe SSDs. When combined with up to 7.5W supplied by the host USB-C port, the enclosure delivers a total of up to 25W of power, suitable for high-draw NVMe devices as recommended by our supplier.

    Tool-Free Snap-In Installation

    Install in seconds with no tools, screws or hassle - just pop open the enclosure, insert your M.2 NVMe SSD or other M.2 PCIe cards, and you’re ready to go. Ideal for workflows that change frequently.

    Premium Aluminum Design That Stays Cool

    The aluminum body features built-in heat dissipation and a thermal pad that draws heat directly from the SSD, keeping performance steady even under heavy load.

    IP55 Dust and Water Resistance


    With the included silicone sleeve, the enclosure meets IP55 protection standards*, shielding it from dust and water splashes - ideal for travel or on-site use.
    *While using silicone protective sleeve. Tested by Hyper’s design team.

    Recycled Materials


    Made with 100% recycled aluminum - sustainable, durable, and built to last.

    FAQs

    Please read our FAQs page to find out more.

    What kind of performance can I expect from this enclosure?

    The HyperDrive Next delivers up to 80Gbps bandwidth over USB4 v2 (Thunderbolt 5 compatible), enabling true PCIe Gen4 x4 speeds for ultra-fast NVMe storage and high-performance PCIe modules.

    What devices and operating systems are supported?

    It works with Mac, Windows PCs, and iPads with USB-C, USB4, or Thunderbolt ports. Supported OS versions include Windows 10+, macOS 12.4+, and iPadOS 13.4+.

    Is this compatible with Thunderbolt-only computers?

    Yes — the enclosure is fully compatible with Thunderbolt 5 and Thunderbolt 4 ports, and also works with USB4 hosts at peak performance.

    Which M.2 SSD sizes and types can I install?

    You can use M.2 SSDs or PCIe modules in sizes 2230, 2242, 2260, and 2280, including PCIe Gen3, Gen4, and Gen5 NVMe drives.

    Can I use this enclosure for modular PCIe cards (e.g., AI modules or networking cards)?

    Yes — unlike many basic enclosures, this supports high-performance PCIe expansion cards such as AI accelerators or networking modules in addition to storage.

    How easy is it to install or swap drives?

    Installation is tool-free — simply open the case, insert your M.2 module, and close. It’s ideal for frequent drive changes or testing multiple devices.
